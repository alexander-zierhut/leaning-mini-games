Teacher Settings

Tenses

Format: emojis(word) … | tense | answer / other answer
Tense is present, past or future. The words in brackets are the hints. Write verbs as to …, e.g. 🍽️(to eat): they get a blue card and the student must put them in the right tense. Several correct answers are separated by /.
The first third of the lines is level 1, the middle third level 2, the last third level 3. Helpers that always keep their word: ➡️ go to, ➕ and, 💭 because, ❌ not, ❓ question.
